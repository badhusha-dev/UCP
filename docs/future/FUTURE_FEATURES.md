## 1. Vision for Platform Evolution

The Universal Commerce Platform v2.0.0 establishes the hardened foundational core: containerized microfrontends, domain microservices, transactional Kafka messaging, and complete observability.

The forward roadmap focuses on:
1. **Third-Party Commercial Ecosystem Integrations**: Real-time payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal, Adyen) and transactional mail relays (SendGrid, AWS SES).
2. **Global Commerce & Localization**: Multi-currency conversion, multilingual catalog localization, and regional tax calculation engines.
3. **Advanced Intelligence & Search**: Debezium Change Data Capture (CDC) syncing catalog updates to Elasticsearch for millisecond semantic vector search.
4. **Omnichannel Fulfillment**: Multi-warehouse routing algorithms, split-shipment dispatch, and automated carrier tracking webhooks.

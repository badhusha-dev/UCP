# Prioritized Feature Backlog

**Universal Commerce Platform**  
*Document Version:* 2.0.0

---

## 1. Prioritized Backlog Items

| Backlog ID | Target Release | Feature Title | Priority | Story Points | User Story Summary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BKL-01** | v2.1.0 | Stripe Payment Gateway Integration | High | 8 | As a shopper, I want to pay securely using Credit Card or Apple Pay via Stripe so that my payment details remain encrypted. |
| **BKL-02** | v2.1.0 | Live SMTP / SendGrid Mailer Driver | High | 5 | As a customer, I want to receive real email order confirmations with delivery estimates. |
| **BKL-03** | v2.1.0 | Redis Server-Side Cart Synchronization | Medium | 5 | As a customer, I want my cart items to follow me from my phone to my desktop laptop when I log in. |
| **BKL-04** | v2.2.0 | Dynamic Coupon & Promo Code Engine | High | 8 | As a merchandiser, I want to create percentage discount codes (`SAVE20`) that apply during checkout. |
| **BKL-05** | v2.2.0 | Debezium CDC Elasticsearch Catalog Sync | Medium | 13 | As a customer, I want typo-tolerant search suggestions as I type in the search bar. |
| **BKL-06** | v2.2.0 | Multi-Currency Conversion (EUR, GBP, CAD)| Medium | 8 | As an international customer, I want to view prices and check out in my local currency. |
| **BKL-07** | v2.3.0 | Multi-Warehouse Inventory Routing | Medium | 13 | As a warehouse manager, I want orders routed to the nearest regional facility holding stock. |
| **BKL-08** | v2.3.0 | Customer Product Reviews & Star Ratings| Low | 5 | As a shopper, I want to read verified customer reviews before making a purchase decision. |

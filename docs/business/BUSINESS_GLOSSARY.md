# Business Glossary

**Universal Commerce Platform**  
*Document Version:* 2.0.0

---

| Business Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AOV (Average Order Value)** | The average monetary amount spent each time a customer places an order on the platform. |
| **Cart Abandonment Rate** | The percentage of shoppers who add items to their virtual cart but exit before completing checkout. |
| **Compensating Action** | An automated reversal operation (e.g. restocking inventory) executed when a downstream step in an order flow fails. |
| **Conversion Funnel** | The customer journey from product page view, to cart addition, to checkout submission, and final order placement. |
| **GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)**| Total monetary value of merchandise sold over a given period across the digital storefront. |
| **Idempotent Order Handling** | Guaranteeing that duplicate customer checkout submissions or replayed Kafka events do not cause duplicate charges or shipments. |
| **Overselling** | The catastrophic business failure where more physical goods are sold to customers than are physically available in inventory. |
| **SKU (Stock Keeping Unit)** | Unique alphanumeric code identifying a distinct physical product variant. |
| **Terminal State** | A permanent final state in the order lifecycle (`DELIVERED` or `CANCELLED`) where no further state mutations are permitted. |
